Hi, I'm Soonho! I design and prototype spatial and tangible experiences across wearables, headsets, and all sorts of other devices.

I’m currently the system UX lead for the Glasses project at Samsung, working with Google on the AndroidXR experience for display and nondisplay glasses.

Previously, I designed 3D procedural authoring tools at Adobe, prototyped intent-based computing at Brain, drove 3D interactions for self-driving vehicles at Cruise Automation, and prototyped solutions for emerging technologies at IDEO CoLab.

When not designing, you can find me skateboarding, climbing, or jotting down something on my field notes.
